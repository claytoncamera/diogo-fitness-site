Coaching in English or Portuguese

A Brazilian online fitness coach, in English or Portuguese.

I’m Diogo Camera, a Brazilian fitness coach based in South Florida. I coach in English, and in Portuguese if you’d rather. Coaching is one-to-one and fully online: your workouts in the Trainerize app, two weigh-ins a week, progress photos every two weeks, a weekly check-in call and direct access for questions. I coach clients anywhere.

In short

Who this is for, and how it runs.

For: Brazilians and other Portuguese speakers, in the US or anywhere, who want Portuguese as an option, alongside English, when they talk about their training, with one person writing the plan and changing it every week.

Not for: Anyone who wants a trainer beside them for every rep, anyone training around pain or a condition a doctor is managing, or anyone who wants a promised outcome by a date.

How it runs: Diogo Fitness (Diogo Fit) is one-to-one online fitness coaching from Diogo Camera, based in South Florida, for clients anywhere in the world. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app; you weigh in twice a week and send progress photos every two weeks; we talk on a weekly call scheduled around your time zone, and you can message me with questions between calls. How one-to-one online coaching works has the full description; the first step is the application, a short message you write and send yourself.

Why it matters

Coaching runs on what you tell me.

The plan only changes well if I know how the week actually went. That is easier to say in the language you think in.

01

Say it the way you’d say it.

How a set felt, why Tuesday fell apart, what your schedule really looks like next week: the details that change the plan come out more easily in your own language.

02

Questions don’t wait for the right words.

Between calls you can message me with questions, in English or Portuguese. Send a video of a lift if you want notes on it.

03

The same coaching either way.

Your workouts in the Trainerize app, two weigh-ins a week, progress photos every two weeks and a weekly call. Only the language we talk in changes.

Fit

Who this is for. And who it isn’t.

This is for you if…

  • You’d like Portuguese as an option. Coaching runs in English, but if a hard week, a lift that felt wrong or a schedule that fell apart comes out better in Portuguese, say it in Portuguese.
  • You’re a Brazilian living in the US, or anywhere else. Florida or anywhere else in the US, or outside the US: coaching is online, and the weekly call is scheduled around your time zone.
  • You switch between both languages. That’s fine. English is the default, and Portuguese is there when it says it better.
  • You want one person who knows your plan. The same person writes it, reads your numbers and changes it, every week, and you talk it through in English or Portuguese.

Not a fit if…

  • You want someone beside you for every rep. Then a trainer at your gym is the better fit. (The honest comparison.)
  • Something hurts, or a doctor is managing a condition. Doctor or physical therapist first. With their OK, coaching can follow. I’m a fitness coach, not a doctor.
  • You want a promised outcome by a date. Results vary and are never guaranteed, in any language. Be careful with anyone who promises one.

How to start

It starts with a message.

The first step is the application: a few quick questions about your goals and your routine, which prepare a short message you copy and send me yourself on Instagram. You can answer the questions in Portuguese. Nothing gets sent until you press send.

Next, we hop on a call about your fitness goals, in English or Portuguese. If I believe I can help you, I’ll walk you through how my coaching works. If I’m not the right coach for you, I’ll be honest about that too.

One plan. One person who knows it. Talked through in English or Portuguese.

Questions

Questions about coaching in Portuguese.

Short answers. The rest is on the main questions page.

Do you coach in Portuguese?

Yes, as an option. I’m Brazilian, and I coach mostly in English; if you’re more comfortable in Portuguese, the weekly call and messages can be in Portuguese. The coaching itself is the same either way: your program in the Trainerize app, two weigh-ins a week, progress photos every two weeks, a weekly call and messages between calls.

Can we mix English and Portuguese?

Yes. English is the default, and you can switch to Portuguese whenever it says it better. What matters is that you can tell me how the week really went.

Do I need to live in Florida or South Florida?

No. I’m based in South Florida, but coaching is fully online and I coach clients anywhere, in the US or outside it. The weekly call is scheduled around your time zone. If you are in South Florida too, the South Florida page covers training around the heat and the rainy season here.

Can I answer the application in Portuguese?

Yes. The application asks a few short questions, and you can answer them in Portuguese. It prepares a message that you copy and paste into Instagram yourself. Nothing is sent until you press send.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the level of support you need and how long we work together. I’d rather talk with you first and then go over the options that actually fit, than hand you a price list for a plan that isn’t built for you. That conversation happens in the DMs. For how to compare quotes from any coach, see how much online fitness coaching costs.
