The approach

Three ideas behind everything I do.

Most programs ask you to rebuild your life around the plan. I do the opposite.

Coaching

Small habits, built around your life

  • I don’t flip your entire routine upside down
  • Consistency beats perfection, and small things add up
  • Fitness should support your life, not consume it
  • Accountability matters because motivation alone is unreliable
  • I teach you. I don’t create permanent dependence
Training

Effective, personal and sustainable

  • Personalized to your goals, schedule, experience and lifestyle
  • Delivered through Trainerize so you can see and track every session
  • Effective sessions over unnecessarily long ones
  • Progressive training and technique that fit you
  • A schedule realistic enough to keep, and the reasoning behind it
Nutrition

Practical, flexible and real-life

  • Calories and protein as practical tools
  • Recipes and meal ideas to work from
  • No banned foods and no crazy meal plan
  • Roughly 80/20, with room for weekends, restaurants and social events
  • Exact ingredient quantities and portions when they help
  • Skills you keep after coaching ends

Your week

Fitness should fit your life, not replace it.

You don’t need to train five or six days a week to make progress. Once a week at the gym is better than none. Pick what you can really do and I’ll build around it.

How many days can you realistically train?

Three is a common starting point.

Full-body sessions or a simple rotation. Enough frequency to keep progressing, with enough rest in between to keep showing up.

  1. Mon Train
  2. Tue Life Weigh-in
  3. Wed Train
  4. Thu Life
  5. Fri Life Weigh-in
  6. Sat Train
  7. Sun Life Check-in call

An example, not a prescription. Your real plan is built around your goal, your experience and the week you actually have.

Fit

Who this is for. And who it isn’t.

This is for you if…

  • You’ve tried to get in shape before and keep falling off.
  • You want fat loss, muscle, strength, performance, or some mix of them.
  • You want a plan that fits your schedule instead of taking over your life.
  • You’re tired of extreme diets, random workouts and routines built for someone else.
  • You’re willing to show up and take responsibility for your part.
  • You want to learn how to manage this yourself, for good.
  • Comfort-seeking, partying, substances or hours of scrolling tend to get in the way of your consistency.

Probably not a fit if…

  • You’re looking for a miracle without the effort.
  • You don’t want to be part of the process.
  • You want extreme crash-diet methods.
  • You’re looking for addiction or mental-health treatment. That’s a job for a licensed professional, and I’m not one.

Progress

How we’ll know it’s working.

Real progress shows up in more than one place. These are the four I track with you.

  • The scale trend

    Two weigh-ins a week, so we’re watching a trend instead of a single number.

    Because the mirror and the scale don’t always tell the same story.

  • Strength

    The weights and reps in your workouts, session after session.

  • Consistency

    How reliably you show up. This is the one that decides everything else.

Questions

Before you apply.

The short answers to what people ask most.

All questions

What’s included in coaching?

A plan built around your schedule, goals and lifestyle. Structured workouts in Trainerize. Personalized nutrition targets and guidance. Two weigh-ins a week and progress photos. A weekly check-in call. Progress reviews and adjustments when something needs to change. Direct access to me for workout, nutrition and accountability questions. And the reasoning behind all of it, so you understand the system.

See how it works, step by step.

How do check-ins and progress tracking work?

You weigh in twice a week and send progress photos. We get on a call every week to look at how things are going, what’s working and what isn’t. When something needs to change, we change it. Your workouts and nutrition are tracked as well, where that helps.

Will I depend on you forever?

No. That’s the opposite of the goal. I explain why the system is built the way it is so you can eventually run it yourself, and you keep access to your workouts after coaching ends.

Do I need to train five or six days a week?

No, and please don’t plan on it. Once a week at the gym is better than none, and how often you train should fit your goal and your schedule. Most people make real progress on less than they expect. We pick the number you’ll actually do and build from there.

Do I have to give up the foods I love?

No. There are no banned foods and no crazy meal plan. We use calories and protein as practical tools and build a flexible structure, roughly 80/20, with room for weekends, restaurants, birthdays and your favorite foods. Pizza stays.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Results depend on the work you put in and on how your body responds. What I can give you is a clear plan, honest feedback and steady support, and I’ll adjust the plan when something isn’t working.
