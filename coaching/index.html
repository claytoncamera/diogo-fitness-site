In short

Who this is for, and how it runs.

For: People who keep starting over and want fitness to fit the life they already have: a plan, someone checking in, and skills that last.

Not for: Anyone after a quick fix or an extreme diet, anyone not ready to take part, or anyone who needs specialized addiction or mental health treatment.

How it runs: Diogo Fitness (Diogo Fit) is one-to-one online fitness coaching from Diogo Camera, based in South Florida, for clients anywhere in the world. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app; you weigh in twice a week and send progress photos every two weeks; we talk on a weekly call scheduled around your time zone, and you can message me with questions between calls. The first step is the application: a short message you write and send yourself, then a call where we talk about your goals.

The approach

Three ideas behind everything I do.

Most programs ask you to rebuild your life around the plan. I do the opposite.

Coaching

Small habits. Real structure. Lasting change.

  • Built around your life. We work with your schedule, not against it.
  • Consistency over perfection. Small actions add up when you repeat them.
  • Fitness that fits your life. Your routine should support your life, not take it over.
  • Accountability that keeps you moving. Motivation comes and goes. Having a plan helps you stay on track.
  • Progress toward independence. I’ll teach you how to take care of yourself so you can maintain your results long-term.
Training

Personalized training. Built for real life.

  • Built around you. Your goals, experience, schedule, and lifestyle shape your program.
  • A plan you can follow. Access your workouts through the Trainerize app, track your sessions, and see your progress over time.
  • Train smarter, not longer. Effective workouts that respect your time without sacrificing results.
  • Progress with purpose. Structured training, proper technique, and progressive overload tailored to your abilities.
  • A routine you can sustain. Know what to do, why you’re doing it, and how to keep progressing long-term.
Nutrition

Flexible nutrition. No unnecessary restrictions.

  • Focus on what matters. Learn to use calories and protein to support your goals without overcomplicating food.
  • Simple meal ideas. Get recipes and practical meal suggestions that fit your preferences and lifestyle.
  • No forbidden foods. No extreme meal plans or unnecessary restrictions. Learn how to make your favorite foods work for you.
  • Balance over perfection. Follow an approach that leaves room for restaurants, weekends, and social events.
  • Practical portions. Get ingredient quantities and portion guidance when they help you stay on track.
  • Skills that last. Build the knowledge and confidence to manage your nutrition long after coaching ends.

Your week

Fitness should fit around your lifestyle, not replace it.

You don’t need to live in the gym to make progress. Even one workout a week is a starting point. We’ll build a routine around your schedule, your goals, and what you can realistically maintain. Online personal training, online coach, online fitness coaching: is there a difference?

People search for this under different names: online personal training, virtual personal training, online personal trainer, online fitness coaching, online coach, online gym coach, online workout coach, virtual fitness coaching, online personal coaching. Here they mean the same service: one person (me) writes your training program, checks in with you every week and changes the plan when your week changes. You do the sessions on your own, at your gym or at home, with the workouts in Trainerize. Names don’t tell you what’s included, so check the table above, whoever you hire.

If you want someone beside you for every rep, a trainer in the room is the better fit, and online coach vs. a trainer at your gym goes through that choice. What online fitness coaching is covers the rest.

Fit

Who this is for. And who it isn’t.

This is for you if…

  • You keep starting over. You’ve tried getting in shape before, but struggle to stay consistent.
  • You have goals you’re ready to work toward. Whether that’s losing fat, building muscle, getting stronger, improving performance, or a mix of them.
  • You want fitness to fit your life. Not a routine that takes over your schedule.
  • You’re tired of guessing. You want a clear plan instead of random workouts, extreme diets, and approaches that don’t fit your needs.
  • You’re ready to take responsibility. I’ll provide the structure and guidance, but you’re willing to put in the work.
  • You want skills that last. Not just short-term results, but the ability to manage your training, nutrition, and habits independently.
  • You want to break unhealthy patterns. Maybe comfort-seeking, partying, substance use, or endless scrolling keeps getting in the way of the person you want to become.

Not a fit if…

  • You’re looking for a quick fix. Lasting results take effort, patience, and consistency.
  • You’re not ready to participate. I’ll guide and support you, but your effort matters too.
  • You want extreme dieting. My approach is built around sustainable habits, not crash diets or unnecessary restrictions.
  • You need specialized addiction or mental health treatment. My coaching supports fitness, nutrition, and healthy lifestyle habits. It isn’t a substitute for care from a licensed healthcare or mental health professional.

Progress

How we’ll know it’s working.

Progress you can measure. Habits you can build. Real progress goes beyond a number on the scale. We’ll track four key areas to understand what’s working, where you’re improving, and what needs adjusting.

  • Your weight trend

    Two weigh-ins per week help us monitor your progress over time without overreacting to normal daily fluctuations.

  • Your physical progress

    Progress photos help us see changes in your physique that the scale alone might not capture.

  • Your strength

    We’ll track your weights, reps, and performance over time to see how you’re progressing in the gym.

  • Your consistency

    Are you following your plan and building habits you can maintain? Consistency helps turn your efforts into lasting progress.

Questions

Before you apply.

A few quick answers to questions you might have.

All questions

What’s included in coaching?

A plan built around your goals, schedule, and lifestyle. You’ll get structured workouts in Trainerize, personalized nutrition guidance, two weigh-ins per week, progress photos, a weekly check-in call, and adjustments based on your progress. You’ll also have direct access to me for questions about training, nutrition, and staying consistent.

Most importantly, I’ll explain the reasoning behind the plan so you understand how to make it work long-term. See how it works, step by step.

Do you coach people outside South Florida, or outside the US?

Yes. Coaching is fully online, so you can be in South Florida, another state or another country. Your workouts live in Trainerize, check-ins happen over messages, and we schedule the weekly call around your time zone. Here’s how online coaching works.

How do check-ins and progress tracking work?

You’ll weigh in twice a week and send progress photos so we can monitor your progress over time. We’ll also have a weekly call to talk about what’s working, what isn’t, and what needs to change. Your workouts and nutrition will help us make decisions based on your progress instead of guessing. Here’s what the first month looks like, week by week.

Do you give meal plans?

I give practical structure instead of a rigid plan: calorie and protein targets, recipes and meal ideas, and portion guidance. You can adapt it to what’s in your fridge and what your week looks like.

Will I depend on you forever?

No, you won’t depend on me forever. That’s the opposite of the goal. I’ll teach you how to manage your training, nutrition, and habits independently, so you can keep moving forward even after coaching ends.

Do I need to train five or six days a week?

Not at all. You don’t need to train five or six days a week or live in the gym to make progress. Even one workout a week is a starting point, and we’ll build a routine around your goals and what you can realistically maintain.

Do I have to give up the foods I love?

No, you don’t have to give up the foods you love. There are no forbidden foods or crazy meal plans. We’ll use calories and protein as practical tools and build a flexible approach that leaves room for restaurants, weekends, birthdays, and your favorite foods.

Pizza stays.

Can you guarantee results?

No, I can’t guarantee results. Be careful with anyone who promises that. Your results depend on many factors, including your consistency, recovery, and how your body responds. What I can promise is a clear plan, honest feedback, support, and adjustments when needed.
