How it started

From going in circles to finding direction.

The short version of my story, in four moments. Read the full story here.

  1. The wake-up call

    The beach

    I got way higher than I intended, and my parents had to come pick me up. I was embarrassed, but it forced me to take an honest look at my life and the direction I was heading. I knew something had to change.

  2. Day one

    A decision to change

    The next day, I went back to the gym. But this time, I knew I needed to change more than my physique. It started with my mindset and the decision to stop living the way I had been.

  3. Building momentum

    Small habits, repeated

    I started showing up consistently, improving my nutrition, and making better decisions. Little by little, I lost 35 pounds, built muscle, and developed the discipline and confidence I had been missing.

  4. Today

    Turning experience into purpose

    Now, I help others build structure, develop healthy habits, and break out of the cycles that keep holding them back. Because lasting change isn’t about motivation alone. It’s about building a system you can stick to, one step at a time.
