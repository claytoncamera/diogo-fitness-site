Getting started

How do I apply?

Use the Apply page. It asks a few short questions about your goal and your week (about two minutes) and turns your answers into a message you send to Diogo on Instagram. Nothing is sent or stored until you press send. Prefer to skip it? Just DM TRANSFORM.

What happens when I apply or DM TRANSFORM?

You send the word, with or without answers. I reply, and we have a normal conversation: what you want, what your week actually looks like, what you’ve tried before and why it didn’t stick. No script and no pressure.

If coaching looks like a good fit, I’ll walk you through the right level and length for you. If it doesn’t, I’ll tell you straight. Here’s how to apply.

I always start strong and then quit. Is this really for me?

It’s exactly who I built this for. Starting over usually isn’t a willpower problem. It’s a structure problem: a plan made for someone with nothing else going on, nobody checking in, and motivation doing all the heavy lifting.

I know the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it, because I lived in it. So the work is building the structure and staying on the other end of it with you.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the level of support you need and how long we work together. I’d rather talk with you first and then go over the options that actually fit, than hand you a price list for a plan that isn’t built for you. That conversation happens in the DMs.

Is the coaching fully online?

Yes. I’m based in South Florida and coaching happens online. Your workouts live in Trainerize, and we stay in touch through direct messages and a weekly check-in call.

Coaching and support

What’s included in coaching?

A plan built around your schedule, goals and lifestyle. Structured workouts in Trainerize. Personalized nutrition targets and guidance. Two weigh-ins a week and progress photos. A weekly check-in call. Progress reviews and adjustments when something needs to change. Direct access to me for workout, nutrition and accountability questions. And the reasoning behind all of it, so you understand the system.

See how it works, step by step.

How do check-ins and progress tracking work?

You weigh in twice a week and send progress photos. We get on a call every week to look at how things are going, what’s working and what isn’t. When something needs to change, we change it. Your workouts and nutrition are tracked as well, where that helps.

Will I depend on you forever?

No. That’s the opposite of the goal. I explain why the system is built the way it is so you can eventually run it yourself, and you keep access to your workouts after coaching ends.

Can you guarantee results?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Results depend on the work you put in and on how your body responds. What I can give you is a clear plan, honest feedback and steady support, and I’ll adjust the plan when something isn’t working.

Training

Do I need to train five or six days a week?

No, and please don’t plan on it. Once a week at the gym is better than none, and how often you train should fit your goal and your schedule. Most people make real progress on less than they expect. We pick the number you’ll actually do and build from there.

I’m a beginner and don’t know my way around a gym. Is that okay?

That’s fine. Your program tells you what to do and in what order, and I teach you the why and the technique as we go. The goal is that the gym stops feeling like a place where everyone else knows something you don’t.

What kinds of goals do you coach?

Fat loss, muscle gain, body recomposition, strength, general fitness and performance. Wrestling is part of my own background, so I understand the performance side too. The training is built for your actual goal, not a generic template.

Where do I get my workouts, and do I keep them?

Programs are delivered through Trainerize, so you can see exactly what’s next and track every session. You keep access to your workouts after coaching ends.

Nutrition

Do I have to give up the foods I love?

No. There are no banned foods and no crazy meal plan. We use calories and protein as practical tools and build a flexible structure, roughly 80/20, with room for weekends, restaurants, birthdays and your favorite foods. Pizza stays.

Do I have to weigh and track every meal forever?

No. Tracking calories and protein early on teaches you what your food actually looks like, and I give exact ingredient quantities and portion guidance when it’s useful. The goal is skills you keep, so you can eat well without living in a spreadsheet.

Do you give meal plans?

I give practical structure instead of a rigid plan: calorie and protein targets, recipes and meal ideas, and portion guidance. You can adapt it to what’s in your fridge and what your week looks like.

About Diogo

Who am I actually talking to?

I’m 23, Brazilian, based in South Florida, and I’m studying Health Science at St. Thomas University. I’ve personally been through fat loss, muscle gain, cutting, bulking, strength training, wrestling and nutrition tracking. The best way to judge whether I’m the right coach for you is the first conversation. My story is here.

Do you help with addiction or mental health?

I’m a fitness coach, not an addiction counselor, therapist or doctor, so I can’t treat substance use or mental health conditions. I’m open about my own history because it shaped how I coach, and I will never shame anyone for theirs.

If you’re dealing with something that needs treatment, a licensed professional is the right call. SAMHSA’s free, confidential helpline is 1-800-662-4357.
