Privacy

What this site does with your data.

Short version: very little. Here’s the detail.

What the site collects

There are no accounts and no checkout, and this site has no forms of its own that send your information to a server. The Apply page asks a few questions to write a draft message for you, and it runs entirely in your browser. What you type there isn’t sent to this site, stored or shared. It only builds the text you choose to copy or email, and that text leaves your device only when you send it yourself: by pasting it into Instagram, or, if you choose to email it, from your own email app to diogo@diogofit.com.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under its own privacy statement. This site doesn’t use those logs.

Where you came from

If you arrive through a link that names its source (for example the link in Diogo’s Instagram profile), this site adds a short label such as “instagram-bio” to the addresses of its own pages as you move between them and to the message it drafts for you, and to the link to the application form as a pre-filled answer, so Diogo can see which link brought you. The label travels in the page address only: this site does not store it, and the only other place it goes is the anonymous page-view count described below.

Analytics

This site uses Plausible Analytics to count visits, which pages were read, where visitors came from (a search engine, Instagram, a link) and which buttons were pressed, such as Apply or DM TRANSFORM, in aggregate. To do this your browser loads a small script from plausible.io and sends each count to Plausible’s servers. Plausible says it uses no cookies and no persistent identifiers, never stores your IP address or the raw details of your browser (it keeps the browser, device type and approximate location, such as country), and keeps its data in the European Union. Nothing you type on the Apply page is sent to it: it records how far you got and how long the message was, not what it said; on the coaching page, which training-days option you tried. It also cannot see anything you do on Instagram or in the application form. Plausible’s own privacy policy is at plausible.io/data-policy.

The full application form

If you choose the full application instead of the Apply page, you leave this site: the form is a Google Form run by Diogo and his team, and Google’s privacy policy applies to it. What you type goes to Google and to that form’s responses, which Diogo and the person on his team who runs the form can read. The form asks for your name, email, phone number, Instagram handle, a few questions about your goals and routine, and, only if you choose to tell us, any injuries or limitations. The site may pre-fill one answer, “How did you find me?”, with a word like “instagram-bio” so Diogo knows where you came from; you can change it. Your answers are used to decide whether coaching is a good fit and to get back to you, and for nothing else. This site only counts that the form link was opened, never what you answer.

Booking a call

If Diogo invites you to book a call, scheduling happens on Calendly, which has its own privacy policy and sees the details you give it.

When you send a DM

The Apply and DM buttons lead to Instagram. From that point you’re using Instagram, which has its own privacy policy. Diogo will see what you send and uses it to reply and to work out whether coaching is a good fit. If you email instead, the same applies to your email, which goes from your own email account to Diogo’s inbox. Please don’t send sensitive medical details in public comments. Share what’s relevant in a private message.

If you become a client

Coaching involves information you choose to share, like goals, training history, weight, progress photos and check-ins. That information is used to coach you. Workouts are delivered through Trainerize, which has its own terms and privacy policy. Client information isn’t sold, and progress photos or results are never shared publicly without your written permission.

Embedded media

Videos and images on this site are hosted on the site itself. Links to Instagram, the application form and the booking page open another site in a new tab, and that site’s own privacy policy applies there.

Questions

The fastest way to reach Diogo is a DM on Instagram.
