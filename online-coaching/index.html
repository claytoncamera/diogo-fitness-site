Online fitness coaching, state by state

These pages exist so that someone in any state can see what a training week looks like where they live, in that state’s own numbers, and how coaching works from a distance. The format is the same everywhere; what changes is the week the plan has to survive. Diogo is based in South Florida and coaches online.

What online coaching means here

Online coaching, on this site, is one-to-one: one coach, one client, one program. It is not a template sold by an app and it is not a group. The program is built for your goal, your schedule and your experience, and it is coached from a distance with weekly check-ins, so one bad week does not undo the plan. How coaching works from a distance

Diogo is based in South Florida and coaches online. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app, you weigh in twice a week, you send progress photos every two weeks, and we talk on a weekly call — wherever you are, scheduled around your time zone. Between calls, you can message me with questions.

Is online coaching the same as online fitness coaching?

Here, yes. Both names mean the same service: training coached from a distance, one-to-one, with the program, the check-ins and the weekly call described above. What online fitness coaching actually is explains what to expect from any online coach.

Does it matter which state I live in?

Not for the coaching itself. The program, the check-ins and the weekly call work the same way in every state, and the call is scheduled in your time zone. What changes is the week the plan has to fit: your commute, your climate, your gym. That is what each state page describes.

Do I need a trainer close to where I live?

No. Online personal training works wherever you live: your workouts are in the Trainerize app, check-ins happen over messages, and the weekly call is scheduled in your time zone. You train at your own gym or at home.

How do I start?

Apply. The form builds a message in about two minutes, and nothing is sent until you press send. The first conversation is mostly questions, and none of them is a test.
