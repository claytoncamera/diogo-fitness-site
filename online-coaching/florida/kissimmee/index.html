How coaching works from a distance

Diogo is based in South Florida and coaches online. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app, you weigh in twice a week, you send progress photos every two weeks, and we talk on a weekly call — Kissimmee is on Eastern time, the same as South Florida, so the call can go after the drive home with no time-zone math. Between calls, you can message me with questions.

I already drive over half an hour each way. Can I train at home instead of driving to a gym?

Yes, and with a commute like that it is often the better plan. The average trip to work here is more than half an hour, so a gym that adds another drive turns one session into a whole evening out. A home setup removes that trip: adjustable dumbbells, a bench or a sturdy step, and a band cover a full-body program three days a week, and the session can start a few minutes after you walk in the door. If you want one heavier day, that is the gym day, on the route home. Broadband is in more than nine households in ten here, so the weekly video call works from the same room. A dumbbell workout plan for home is where the home version starts.

Can I still walk outside after work in July?

Yes, at the right hour. At the Orlando International Airport station, the nearest full-record one, the July high is above ninety and more than a hundred days a year reach ninety or more. On those days the walk goes after sunset rather than straight off the drive, or turns into a few minutes of indoor work instead: step-ups, carries and a short circuit in the living room. When January lows around fifty come back, the walk can go wherever the day allows. The walk is the easy part of the plan to move; the lifting stays where it is.
