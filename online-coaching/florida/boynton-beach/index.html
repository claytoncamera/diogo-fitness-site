Age-adjusted, an estimated 25.2% of adults in Boynton Beach report no leisure-time physical activity (a modeled estimate, 95% CI 22.0 to 28.8). An estimated 39.4% usually sleep less than 7 hours in a 24-hour period (same kind of estimate, 95% CI 34.2 to 44.6).

How coaching works from a distance

Diogo is based in South Florida and coaches online. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app, you weigh in twice a week, you send progress photos every two weeks, and we talk on a weekly call — Boynton Beach is on Eastern time, the same as me, so the call sits in the evening or on a weekend morning, whichever the week allows. Between calls, you can message me with questions.

Almost everyone I know drives to work. What does an online plan look like for that week?

Three sessions in slots the job cannot take. With about one worker in ten at home and a trip to work of under half an hour, the plan does not count on a midday gap. It uses the morning before the drive for one session, the first hour home for another, and a weekend morning for the longest one; the weekday sessions are short and built around two or three main lifts so they finish before the evening does. The weekly call sits in the evening or on the weekend, whichever the week leaves free, and that is where next week’s three slots are set. A 30-minute workout that still counts is the weekday version.

The weather station is a wildlife refuge with a partial record. Can I plan by it?

Yes, for the shape of the year. The refuge station is the nearest one with the four normals this page uses, about fourteen kilometres inland, and each of its values rests on twenty-one to twenty-three years rather than thirty, which the flags above say. For training that is enough: more than sixty days a year at or above ninety degrees means the hot-month outdoor work goes early or after dark and the lifting stays indoors; January lows in the mid-fifties mean the cool months are open at any hour. The city is on the coast and the station is inland, which the distance above says; the plan does not change for a degree or two either way.
