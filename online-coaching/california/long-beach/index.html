Activity and sleep in Long Beach

Age-adjusted, an estimated 25.8% of adults in Long Beach report no leisure-time physical activity (a modeled estimate, 95% CI 23.6 to 28.0). An estimated 37.3% usually sleep less than 7 hours in a 24-hour period (same kind of estimate, 95% CI 34.4 to 40.1).

Daily life in Long Beach, by the numbers

From the Census Bureau's American Community Survey, published value and margin of error together: about 16.2% of workers who live here work from home (90% margin of error plus or minus 0.8); commuters average 29.9 minutes to work (90% margin of error plus or minus 0.5); the median age is 37.1 years (90% margin of error plus or minus 0.4); 93.3% of households have a broadband subscription (90% margin of error plus or minus 0.5).

Climate in Long Beach

At the LONG BEACH DAUGHERTY FLD station (USW00023129, 4.0 km from the Census internal point), the 1991–2020 normals are a July high of 81.4 F, a January low of 46.9 F, 23.3 days a year at or above 90 F, and 12.02 inches of rain a year. Partial records behind some of these: January low 23 years (flag R, representative: at least 10 but fewer than 24 years).

How coaching works from a distance

Diogo is based in South Florida and coaches online. Your workouts live in the Trainerize app, you weigh in twice a week, you send progress photos every two weeks, and we talk on a weekly call — for Long Beach that is Pacific time, three hours behind me, so the call usually lands in your early evening or my early morning. Between calls, you can message me with questions.

The weather is mild all year. What actually gets in the way?

The week, not the weather. The station figures above give Long Beach a mild July and only a few weeks a year at or above 90 F, so the weather rarely cancels an outdoor session. What breaks routines here is the schedule: a commute close to half an hour each way, on top of the workday. So the plan books sessions at the same point in the day, before work or straight after, and keeps a short version for the days that run long. The weekly call is about where next week’s sessions go, not about the season. That is a scheduling problem, and scheduling is something a weekly call can fix.

About a quarter of adults here report no activity. Is coaching for beginners?

Yes, and that is where a plan changes the most. The CDC’s estimate above says about a quarter of adults in Long Beach report no leisure-time physical activity; it is a model-based estimate, so read it as the backdrop. A beginner does not need a harder program, they need one they understand: two sessions a week to start, five or six movements explained before they are loaded, weights that leave several reps in reserve, and a first month built so that nothing leaves you too sore to come back. The weekly call is there for every question the first month raises.
