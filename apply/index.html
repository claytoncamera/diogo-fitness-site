What happens when I apply or DM TRANSFORM?

When you apply or DM TRANSFORM, you send the word, with or without answers. I reply, and we have a normal conversation: what you want, what your week actually looks like, what you’ve tried before and why it didn’t stick. No script and no pressure.

If coaching looks like a good fit, I’ll walk you through the right level and length for you. If it doesn’t, I’ll tell you straight. Here’s how to apply.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the level of support you need and how long we work together. I’d rather talk with you first and then go over the options that actually fit, than hand you a price list for a plan that isn’t built for you. That conversation happens in the DMs. Is coaching worth it for you at all? Start there.

I always start strong and then quit. Is this really for me?

If you always start strong and then quit, you’re exactly who I built this coaching for. Starting over usually isn’t a willpower problem. It’s a structure problem: a plan made for someone with nothing else going on, nobody checking in, and motivation doing all the heavy lifting.

I know the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it, because I lived in it. So the work is building the structure and staying on the other end of it with you. Not sure a coach is worth it for you? Here’s how I’d decide.
