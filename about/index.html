From the video, in my words. Recorded June 2026.

What’s up guys, Diogo here. A lot of you have seen my transformation over the years, but not everyone knows the story behind it.

I’m Brazilian, 22 years old, and I’m an online coach, but trust me, I wasn’t always the guy that you see today. I had a rough time and I was stuck. I struggled with procrastination, bad influences, and I also got caught up on drugs. Every single day, I just felt like I was watching my life slip away from the person who I truly wanted to become.

One day, I stopped making excuses. I realized nobody was coming to save me, and that if my life was going to change, I needed to take action.

And so what did I do? I started small. I implemented small habits into my life. I showed up to the gym every single day, even if I didn’t feel like going. I cleaned up my diet, I stayed consistent. And those small decisions that I took not only transformed my body, but it transformed my life.

And now, my mission is to help others do the same. If you’re tired of starting over, tired of making excuses, and you’re ready to become the person that you know you’re capable of being, then send me a DM with the word transform, and let’s get to work.

I

Comfort first

Before fitness, I was unhealthy, unstructured and chasing whatever felt good right now. Marijuana, kratom, nicotine, alcohol, hours of scrolling, nights at the kava bar.

I told myself I was young. That this is what being young is for, and that I’d get serious later.

II

The beach

One day at the beach I got way higher than I meant to. I stopped surfing and, for the first time in a long while, actually thought about where my life was going.

I was overweight. I had no real goals. I was surrounded by people who weren’t pushing me anywhere. And I was so out of it that my parents had to come pick me up, because I couldn’t take care of myself.

That was embarrassing. It was also exactly what I needed. The embarrassment made me take an honest look at myself.

III

The next day

The next day I went back to the gym, and I stayed consistent.

Here’s the strange part. I already knew about training and dieting. I’d just written it all off, because I thought what I was doing was freedom.

It wasn’t. It was a loop of cheap dopamine, comfort, short-term pleasure and wasted potential.

IV

What it gave me

Fitness gave me back a lot of things I didn’t know I was missing.

  • Structure
  • Purpose
  • Self-respect
  • Community
  • Mental clarity
  • Confidence

It also gave me proof. Action plus discipline can change your life. If I can change, then so can others.

V

Why I coach the way I do

I know the gap between knowing what to do and actually living it, because I stood in it. That’s why I don’t flip your whole routine upside down. I build small habits around the life you already have. Consistency beats perfection, and small things add up.

I also don’t want you to need me forever. The point is that you understand the system well enough to take care of yourself.

On the mat

Training isn’t only about how you look.

Wrestling is part of my background, so performance is something I understand, not just fat loss and muscle. If your goal is to get stronger, faster or better at a sport, that’s a goal I’m glad to coach.
