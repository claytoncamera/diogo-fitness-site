No hype and no miracle claims. Just the things I’d tell you if we were talking in the gym.

Consistency Why you keep starting over (and how to stop) Starting over usually isn't a willpower problem. It's a system problem: decide ahead, shrink the plan for bad weeks, never miss twice, have a witness. 3 min read Consistency Consistency beats motivation. Every time. Motivation comes and goes, so plan for the days you don't feel like it: set days and times, a smaller workout on bad days, and never miss twice. 3 min read Nutrition Calories and protein: the two numbers that matter most If you lift, aim for 0.7 to 1 gram of protein per pound of bodyweight a day. For calories, estimate your maintenance, pick a direction and adjust. 3 min read Nutrition The 80/20 approach: eat flexibly without wrecking your progress About 80% of what you eat is filling, protein-rich, mostly unprocessed food. The other 20% is food you simply enjoy. Calories and protein still count. 3 min read Training How often should you train? Less than you think. Once a week beats none. General fitness and fat loss are happy on two or three days a week. Pick the schedule you can repeat, then make it count. 3 min read Fat loss Why can't I lose fat? Six common reasons Eating healthy but not losing weight? Six things I'd check first: portions, underestimated intake, weekends, steps, consistency and one-day weigh-ins. 3 min read Fat loss Walking is the most underrated tool for fat loss Don't jump to a big number. Start from the steps you already average, add a bit and hold it until it feels routine. Then raise it. 2 min read Training There's always 3 reps in reserve Reps in reserve (RIR) is how many more good reps you could have done before failure. For most working sets, aim for about 1 to 3 in reserve. 2 min read
