Who lifts? Strength training by age and sex, from the CDC

One CDC survey question, asked of hundreds of thousands of adults: do you do activities to strengthen your muscles on two or more days a week? Here's how the answer changes with age and sex, and how the national figure has moved since 2011.

Short answer: In the CDC's 2023 survey, 41.0% of U.S. adults said they do muscle-strengthening activities on 2 or more days a week: 51.2% of 18- to 24-year-olds, falling to 37.5% at 55 to 64 and 38.1% at 65 and older. Men reported it more than women, 45.1% against 37.0%.2

The CDC reports the share of U.S. adults who do muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days a week, from its Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System. Two days is the line because it's the guideline: the WHO asks adults to do muscle-strengthening activity on 2 or more days a week.1 This page puts the national answers in one table, by age group and by sex, with the confidence interval and the number of people asked for each.

What the numbers show

  • Fewer than half of adults report it. 41.0% of adults said they strength-train on two or more days a week in 2023.2
  • The youngest group reports it most. 51.2% at 18 to 24, against 37.5% to 38.3% between 45 and 64.2
  • It doesn't keep falling after 55. The 65-and-older group (38.1%) sits at about the same level as 45 to 64.2
  • Men report it more than women, 45.1% against 37.0%.2

These are self-reported answers, not measured training. They tell you how common the habit is, not how hard or how well anyone trains.

Common questions

What percentage of adults strength train?

In the CDC's 2023 survey, 41.0% of U.S. adults said they do muscle-strengthening activities on 2 or more days a week.2

How many women lift weights?

37.0% of women in the 2023 survey reported muscle-strengthening activities on 2 or more days a week, against 45.1% of men.2 The question covers any muscle-strengthening activity, not only weights. Strength training for women covers how to start.

Do older adults strength train less?

Less than 18- to 24-year-olds, but about the same as ages 45 to 64: 38.1% at 65 and older, against 38.3% at 45 to 54 and 37.5% at 55 to 64.2 Workouts for seniors covers the guideline for older adults.
