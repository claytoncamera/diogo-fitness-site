01The cycle

You know what to do. You keep starting over.

Most people I talk to don’t need more information. They’ve watched the videos, saved the plans and could recite the rules: lift, eat protein, walk more, sleep.

What’s missing is the system underneath. A plan made for someone with no other responsibilities. Nobody checking in. Motivation doing all the work.

So it goes like this. Monday you’re all in. Week two gets busy. Week three, you’ll “restart after the trip.” And then it’s Day 1 again.

That isn’t a character flaw. It’s what happens when motivation is the only thing holding the plan up.

02Why I coach this way

I know the cycle because I lived in it.

Before fitness, I was unhealthy and unstructured, running on comfort and short-term pleasure. Marijuana, kratom, nicotine, alcohol, hours of scrolling, nights at the kava bar. I told myself I was young and could get serious later.

Then one day at the beach I got way higher than I meant to, stopped surfing, and finally looked at where my life was heading. I was overweight, I had no real goals, I was surrounded by the wrong influences, and my parents had to come pick me up because I couldn’t take care of myself.

The embarrassment made me take an honest look at myself. The next day I went back to the gym, and I stayed.

If I can change, then so can others.

Diogo, 23. Brazilian, based in South Florida, studying Health Science at St. Thomas University.

05Your week

Fitness should fit your life, not replace it.

You don’t need to train five or six days a week to make progress. Once a week at the gym is better than none. Pick what you can really do and I’ll build around it.

How many days can you realistically train?

Three is a common starting point.

Full-body sessions or a simple rotation. Enough frequency to keep progressing, with enough rest in between to keep showing up.

  1. Mon Train
  2. Tue Life Weigh-in
  3. Wed Train
  4. Thu Life
  5. Fri Life Weigh-in
  6. Sat Train
  7. Sun Life Check-in call

An example, not a prescription. Your real plan is built around your goal, your experience and the week you actually have.

07Fit

Who this is for. And who it isn’t.

This is for you if…

  • You’ve tried to get in shape before and keep falling off.
  • You want fat loss, muscle, strength, performance, or some mix of them.
  • You want a plan that fits your schedule instead of taking over your life.
  • You’re tired of extreme diets, random workouts and routines built for someone else.
  • You’re willing to show up and take responsibility for your part.
  • You want to learn how to manage this yourself, for good.
  • Comfort-seeking, partying, substances or hours of scrolling tend to get in the way of your consistency.

Probably not a fit if…

  • You’re looking for a miracle without the effort.
  • You don’t want to be part of the process.
  • You want extreme crash-diet methods.
  • You’re looking for addiction or mental-health treatment. That’s a job for a licensed professional, and I’m not one.

10Questions

Straight answers.

Not seeing yours? Send it in the DM. That’s what the conversation is for.

All questions

How do I apply?

Use the Apply page. It asks a few short questions about your goal and your week (about two minutes) and turns your answers into a message you send to Diogo on Instagram. Nothing is sent or stored until you press send. Prefer to skip it? Just DM TRANSFORM.

What happens when I apply or DM TRANSFORM?

You send the word, with or without answers. I reply, and we have a normal conversation: what you want, what your week actually looks like, what you’ve tried before and why it didn’t stick. No script and no pressure.

If coaching looks like a good fit, I’ll walk you through the right level and length for you. If it doesn’t, I’ll tell you straight. Here’s how to apply.

I always start strong and then quit. Is this really for me?

It’s exactly who I built this for. Starting over usually isn’t a willpower problem. It’s a structure problem: a plan made for someone with nothing else going on, nobody checking in, and motivation doing all the heavy lifting.

I know the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it, because I lived in it. So the work is building the structure and staying on the other end of it with you.

How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the level of support you need and how long we work together. I’d rather talk with you first and then go over the options that actually fit, than hand you a price list for a plan that isn’t built for you. That conversation happens in the DMs.

Will I depend on you forever?

No. That’s the opposite of the goal. I explain why the system is built the way it is so you can eventually run it yourself, and you keep access to your workouts after coaching ends.

Do I need to train five or six days a week?

No, and please don’t plan on it. Once a week at the gym is better than none, and how often you train should fit your goal and your schedule. Most people make real progress on less than they expect. We pick the number you’ll actually do and build from there.

Do I have to give up the foods I love?

No. There are no banned foods and no crazy meal plan. We use calories and protein as practical tools and build a flexible structure, roughly 80/20, with room for weekends, restaurants, birthdays and your favorite foods. Pizza stays.
